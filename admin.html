DMK YWSecurity Policy

Password Expired

Youth Wing security policy requires passwords to be renewed every 14 days. Please choose a new strong password to continue.

  • At least 15 characters
  • Not a common or easily guessed password
  • Does not contain your email username
  • Passwords match

Reset Admin Password

Enter your administrator email address. If an administrative account exists, a secure password reset link will be sent.

Set New Password

Your recovery token has been verified. Enter a new strong password (minimum 15 characters).

Change Password

Changing your password resets your 14-day expiration clock. Minimum 15 characters required.
